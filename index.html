Home Furnishings · Saudi Arabia

Three Platforms, One Budget, One Story

Goal: a single coordinated read across Meta, TikTok and Google for a furniture showroom, to see which channel actually earns its share of spend.

  • 6.33% Google Search CTR

16–31 Aug 2026 · ~710 SAR total spend across all three platforms, as billed.
