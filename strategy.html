01 · SITUATION

Where Things Stand

Market, competitor, and SWOT analysis — what's actually true about the business today, not the pitch version.

02 · OBJECTIVES

One Measurable Target

A specific, time-bound goal — leads, bookings, app downloads — not "grow brand awareness."

03 · STRATEGY

The Big Decisions

Which audience segments, which channels, which positioning — decided before any creative is written.

04 · TACTICS

Channel By Channel

What each platform is actually for in this plan — Meta for retargeting, TikTok for reach, Google for intent, and so on.

05 · ACTION

A Real Timeline

A phased calendar — awareness, then conversion, then scale — with owners and dates, not just a wishlist.

06 · CONTROL

The KPIs That Matter

The handful of numbers that get checked weekly, agreed before launch, not invented afterward to fit the result.

Strategy In Practice

REAL PROBLEMS, NAMES WITHHELD

These are real strategic engagements. Client names, brand names, specific property names, budgets, and exact tactics are withheld to protect active confidentiality agreements — what's shown below is the shape of the problem and the approach, not the deliverable itself.

Real Estate · United Arab Emirates

Launching A Luxury Residential Project

  • Challenge: a high-end residential developer needed a multi-platform launch, not another generic property listing post.
  • Approach: a weekly content rhythm across Instagram, LinkedIn, and short-form video, with a luxury-editorial tone and a staged, multi-week launch calendar.
  • Detail: captions and messaging were reviewed by the client's own brand team before each post — a sign-off loop built into the workflow, not an afterthought.
Health & Wellness Subscription · UAE

Building Demand Before An App Launch

  • Challenge: a subscription meal-delivery service across three cities needed demand ready before its own app went live.
  • Approach: a three-phase plan — awareness, then direct sales, then search intent — sequenced so each phase funds credibility for the next, with distinct audience segments defined for each phase.
Education & Test Preparation · UAE

Two Audiences, One Budget, Different Messages

  • Challenge: an education provider's test-prep and teacher-training programs needed to reach two very different audiences — students and working teachers — without one message diluting the other.
  • Approach: separate audience segments per program, Meta and TikTok for reach with Snapchat carrying remarketing, and distinct creative tracks per segment rather than one message for everyone.
